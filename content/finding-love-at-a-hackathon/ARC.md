# Finding Love at a Hackathon
## First ten episodes and later phases

The external engine is provisional entry, ethical technical proof and fair authorship. The emotional engine is attraction earned through work, boundaries and accountable choices. Episode ten closes the first technical trial and opens the next trust trial. It does not win the hackathon or settle the relationship.

### Broad phases for the 45-episode plan

| Episodes | External phase | Romantic phase and room for reversal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01–10 | Entry, authorship complaint, offline qualifying failure, bounded repair and restored stage access. Fully scripted. | Curiosity, respect, friction, repair and one unanswered invitation. |
| 11–18 | Separate accounts establish their distinct contributions; the first judged demo reveals that a successful tool can create new expectations. Leo remains a capable competitor, and Maya's work is assessed on evidence. | Support without speaking for the other person. Private rapport conflicts with public attribution and different career aims. |
| 19–27 | Midnight work, a harder service-design test and pressure to overpromise strain the team. A useful limitation may cost a better-looking score. | Vulnerability becomes voluntary. A disagreement has consequences and is repaired through action; attraction does not erase boundaries. |
| 28–36 | The final build and live presentation require each person's independent competence. Rival evidence and fair competition decide the hackathon result rather than a convenient rescue. | A deliberate personal invitation can receive a real answer only after both have made independent choices. Any later intimacy is mutual and earned. |
| 37–45 | Prize or loss has practical consequences: ownership credit, future work and the gap between a promising demo and a useful service. Resolve the primary authorship and event arcs by 45. | Decide whether the relationship survives outside the event. Give the central romance a satisfying chosen direction at 45, not an endless almost-confession. |

### Optional expansion to 60

Episodes 46–60 may follow the agreed post-hackathon pilot: real users challenge assumptions, a new professional opportunity creates distance, and the team must negotiate work and affection beyond a deadline. Keep the outcome of episode 45 intact. Expansion adds consequences and new choices rather than revoking a relationship, repeating Leo's first-page claim, or withholding the original resolution for fifteen more episodes. No episodes beyond ten are fully scripted here.
